2 · Model facts, presence and checkpoint identity

Keep model values synthetic and in memory. Public types must be labeled experimental. Do not model HMAC verification or network admission; input facts are assumed authenticated for a named synthetic scope. All keys include tenant, streamId, epoch. Within a scope, delivery identity binds deliveryId, digest, sequence; a sequence also binds one delivery identity/digest. Checkpoint identity binds checkpointSequence, digest. Different scopes may reuse all local labels.

Expose a small pure reducer applyEvidence(state, fact) and a deterministic summarizeEvidence(state, scope). Store observed facts/conflicts without declaring coverage. Reapplying an identical delivery or checkpoint is a no-op. Same identity with different digest/sequence, or same sequence for another delivery, records a stable conflict. Store enough canonical fact information that applying the same finite set in a different order yields the same summary. No “first winner” may erase a conflict.

Compute the highest contiguous delivered sequence from the finite delivery facts, starting at 1. Preserve checkpoint facts separately; accountedSeq < observedPresence does not by itself mean rollback. The summary may expose sorted IDs, checkpoint identities, presence and conflicts. It must not return complete, verified_zero, a rate or an inferred final frontier.

Keep bounds explicit: at most 64 distinct canonical fact variants per model state, across its scopes. Identical retries consume no slot. On the 65th distinct variant, enter a terminal typed capacity state; summarization then returns only that outcome, with no normal presence or coverage summary. Do not evict a conflict or expose the prior state as a successful result. Convergence is required for finite sets within the bound. Test identical replay at 64 and a contradictory 65th variant. No unbounded production-storage algorithm is being approved. Retention and loss ranges can be explicit fixture facts used by the oracle; production persistence/coalescing semantics remain S0b/S1 work.
