Relevant existing seams
- prepareTelemetryDelivery builds immutable v2 bytes, digest and occurrence time before IdempotencyStore.complete. It returns null when off, unbound, unapproved, unprojectable or grant-mismatched; a construction error can return a coarse gap. Do not assume null means a known zero.
- complete atomically writes the completed result, receipt and outbox-or-gap. Replayed or failed evaluations do not call it. Protected afterCompletionWrites is the commit-failure seam. Current transport identity is random delivery ID; no producer epoch, contiguous sequence, checkpoint or source inventory exists.
- Producer gaps are only (UTC occurrence hour, reason, lost count, expiry), no grade or modality. They are durable but not sent to the receiver. A gap must taint every finer matching period unless a new proof narrows it safely.
- Producer alarm shares cleanup/delivery duties and goes idle without pending work. A never-contacted tenant has no Durable Object state or alarm. There is no scheduled handler or heartbeat roster to wake zero-traffic tenants.
- Receiver counts a whole E/F pair with delivery dedupe in one transaction. The key is tenant + delivery ID hashed with a domain, not an analytics dimension. Current dimensions include chronological 15-minute UTC bucket, source, grade, modality, approved producer revision, trusted config and calendar revision. The maximum accepted occurrence age is 24 hours plus a separate 5-minute future-skew bound.
- W8a strict server config pins effective intervals/zone/calendar/revisions and approved retention. Policy changes only tighten stored expiries; revocation is monotonic and takes effect on next authenticated contact. Never invent evidence for pre-enrollment, expired or revoked periods.

Design decisions to resolve with evidence
A. Proof unit and trust: define the observation scope, tenant/producer stream identity, server-owned expected producer inventory and effective observation windows. Distinguish enabled/disabled, unavailable, incomplete, provisional, complete nonzero and verified zero. Declare whether a worker outage, uninstrumented completion, invalid setting or disabled interval is observable or must remain unavailable. Local collector health is never producer evidence.
B. Durable evidence: compare at least two real designs (for example contiguous producer progress/checkpoints versus durable bucket inventory reconciliation). Explain why the chosen design proves completeness under concurrent commits, duplicate IDs, partial batches, lost acknowledgments, out-of-order delivery, retries, gaps and cold starts. Show storage and transaction boundaries. Avoid per-message analytics ledgers or identities; short-lived protected transport state must be bounded and justified.
C. Empty periods: show exactly how a never-contacted or zero-traffic tenant is activated, how its expected intervals and wakeup schedule become durable, how configuration/approval expiry stops it, and what distinguishes an unobserved hour from verified E=0. Heartbeat alone is insufficient. Do not infer a zero retroactively from a late heartbeat.
D. Checkpoint protocol: specify strict version/path, authentication domain separation, fresh signatures, stable retry identity/digest, durable acknowledgment meaning, stale/conflicting checkpoints, producer epochs/revisions and replay/tombstone retention. Keep v1 and v2 compatibility explicit. Writer keys must never acquire reader access. A reported producer value must be tied to durable source state, not accepted as arbitrary counts.
E. Closure/corrections: define occurrence and observation clocks, watermark/finality, provisional state and late changes. Address a request prepared before a checkpoint but completing after it; an event admitted up to five minutes ahead; config/revision change mid-window; old/new producers overlapping; repeated and skipped DST hours; half-hour zones. State which correction invalidates an earlier completeness assertion and how the receiver learns it.
F. Retention and revocation: name every additional table and TTL origin; reuse approved categories only if their semantics truly cover that evidence. Otherwise require a new explicit policy field with compatibility handling, no default duration. Evidence needed to qualify a retained rollup must not expire first. Cover long-short-long, alarm deletion, read-time filtering, restart, re-enrollment and retention expiry without resurrecting completeness.
G. Exposure/rates: define matching E/F units across source/grade/modality/release/config/calendar/time and coverage. E=0 yields null rate; missing, incomplete or mismatched coverage withholds a rate. F <= E. Multiple expected producers require all qualifying streams; do not compare numerators/denominators across releases.

Verification matrix required in the proposal
- Source commit failure rolls back result/receipt/outbox/evidence together; replay/failed request adds nothing; restart resumes the same stream without invented continuity.
- Zero traffic under a fully observed declared window versus never activated, producer silent while receiver healthy, disabled mid-bucket and after re-enable.
- Missing one expected producer, stale checkpoint, lost/forged/mismatched ack, dedup/conflict, out-of-order progress, producer restart/epoch change, pre-W8b outstanding deliveries.
- Outbox capacity/expiry/construction/revocation/conflict gaps, including a coarse hourly gap touching four quarter-hour buckets; no complete state after an unresolved gap.
- Prepared-before/sealed-after request, delayed old revision after a deploy, clock skew, late correction, both DST folds and half-hour/45-minute zones.
- Policy shortens then lengthens, approval lapses, read/alarm expiry, restart, revocation/re-enrollment and stale replay cannot restore either a count or completeness.
- Cross-tenant/source/grade/revision spoofing; unknown and oversized input; safe integer overflow; no sentinel text, source/request/evaluation IDs, tokens or raw transport IDs in rollup/report/log output.
- Matched nonzero E/F rate, E=0 null, F>E rejected, incomplete/mismatched cohort withheld. No dashboard, suppression or privacy approval claimed.
