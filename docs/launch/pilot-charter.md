Revision W1-2026-10-29 · HM-19 / program HM-16 · **Draft: no host or live exercise authorized**

The current permitted engineering activity is a repository-local synthetic design study. The
instrumentable source is the synthetic evaluation harness and, when implemented, a separately
watermarked demo. No real student content, identities, private messages or institutional records
are inputs. A live or consenting-staff study requires a revised charter naming its actual source,
participants, authority, response path and approvals. Nothing below records a human signature.

## Purpose and one proposed workflow

Test whether a private pre-send prompt and accessible report/help route in **one institution-owned
grades 9–12 project discussion** can reduce wrongful restrictions and improve help-seeking without
exposing private records. This is a proposed workflow, not an existing school integration. The
school must identify an unmet need in its current vendor/referral workflow before choosing it.

Michigan Virtual and Oakland Schools are research/introduction hypotheses. Catholic Central,
Novi and other researched schools are possible hosts, not partners or enrolled customers. A
regional introducer cannot authorize a member school's records. No outreach has been sent.

## Data inventory and exclusions

| Data | Current synthetic study | Before any real exercise |
|---|---|---|
| Text/image submitted for evaluation | Invented fixtures; images subject to existing policy floors | Approved source, purpose, modality, eligibility and notices; student images remain held until separately calibrated |
| Identity, age and authorization | Fictional tenant/context only | Source verifies role, tenant and age eligibility from authoritative records; classifier gets no birth date |
| Source messages/permission events | No school messages or live events | Source controls originals, corrections, withdrawals and lawful access; narrow event contract |
| Evaluation result and usage receipt | Content-free schema; local test storage | Review linkability, access and applicable receipt/idempotency schedule |
| Aggregate telemetry | Disabled for real sources; synthetic fixtures only | Approved retention, coverage, suppression, query policy and target verification |
| Incident evidence and identities | No live case database or identity vault | Choose custody; implement rights/holds and independent disclosure controls first |
| Feedback and research responses | No participant survey deployed | Protocol, notices, accessibility, voluntary participation and retention review |

Exclude counseling/clinical channels, personal accounts/DMs, off-platform collection, biometrics,
general social graphs, automated discipline, reputation scores and open-ended account access.
Personal mute/block is a protected boundary. Reporting must remain reachable if evaluation,
budget, analytics or the proposed nudge is unavailable. A teacher role alone grants no case access.

## Demonstration protocol

Each scenario uses invented actors. A visual mockup must say **synthetic** and identify whether
the behavior is implemented or proposed. The current evaluator cannot establish social intent.

| Fictional case | What to compare | Required interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Quoted language versus direct threat | Context and uncertainty | A quote is not automatically the speaker's threat; retain ambiguity |
| Rumor forwarding versus supportive report | Harmful repetition and help-seeking | Genuine reporting is not punished or blocked from the help route |
| Personal mute versus delegated project-group removal | Personal choice and institutional privilege | Protect personal mute; verify authority/required participation before any group-restoration action |
| Bait-and-report | Multiple possible accounts of the same event | Reports are claims, copied reports are not independent witnesses, and the model cannot assign guilt |

The sequence is synthetic local evaluation → separately approved consenting-staff study → optional
disclosed limited student exercise → independent continue/revise/stop decision. Calendar dates
and commercial estimates in the prospectus do not waive entry gates or promise enrollment.

## Pre-registration record

Freeze the following before collecting outcome data. Numerical limits and sample sizes remain
pending the independent evaluation and safeguarding owners; none are invented here.

| Outcome | Unit and comparison | Required reporting | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wrongful restriction / missed concern | Adjudicated eligible scenario, baseline versus candidate | Counts, abstentions, intervals and subgroup applicability; retain disagreements | Evaluation lead + blinded panel |
| Help-seeking | Eligible attempt to reach the existing help/report path | Completion and blocked/abandoned attempts; outage drills | Safeguarding lead |
| Attribution / appeals | Reviewed claim or challenged decision | Wrong referent, correction, appeal outcome and time | Records + evaluation leads |
| Participation / trust | Voluntary approved research instrument | Response denominator, withdrawal, missing responses and qualitative harm | Independent research owner |
| Human response | Actual referral acknowledged by trained staff | Coverage hours, pending/censored work and fallback failures | Safeguarding operations |
| Total cost | Exact usage receipts plus measured staff labor | Unknown cost, escalation and operating costs; no sampled billing | Finance + technical operator |

Freeze corpus revision, labeling/adjudication rubric, pack/calibration/policy versions, eligible
cohorts, study design, exclusions, sample-size rationale, tolerances, review dates and withdrawal
process. Analyze all enrolled eligible units under that protocol; fewer reports or edited messages
alone do not prove improved welfare. Synthetic/fake-model mechanics are not a measured efficacy
benchmark. Collect no real survey data until its protocol is approved.

## Entry, stop and withdrawal authority

Before real participants: name the sponsor, source owner, records/privacy counsel, security
operator, safeguarding lead and independent evaluation owner; approve lawful basis/notices,
eligibility, data flows, retention, provider route, calibrated applicability, rights, staffing,
accessibility and a tested source rollback/referral path. Use the [launch checklist](launch-checklist.md).

The safeguarding/privacy/security owner can stop enrollment for a missed urgent referral,
unintended disclosure, unapproved route/config drift, unexplained wrongful restriction, identity
error or harm to help-seeking. The product/source owner executes the pause and restores the
existing help path; the records officer coordinates deletion with legal/inspection holds. Restore
service only after the responsible owners record a reviewed decision and repeat affected checks.
