# Hall Monitor: synthetic-first design partnership

W1-2026-10-29 · Two-page briefing, draft for discovery · No host, outreach or purchase commitment

## Page 1 — the proposed study

Hall Monitor is a product-neutral text/image evaluation service for a digital activity that an
institution controls. We propose testing a narrower preventive workflow: a private pre-send prompt
and an accessible help/report path within one authorized grades 9–12 project discussion.
Whether that workflow improves participation or safety is a research question.

The running software evaluates submitted content, applies pinned policies, preserves uncertainty
and returns a decision with content-free usage receipts. The proposed social-context demo uses
four fictional cases: quoted language versus threat, rumor forwarding versus supportive reporting,
personal mute versus delegated project-group removal, and bait-and-report. It must distinguish
implemented evaluation from simulated social reasoning and UI. It cannot establish a child's intent.

We seek a named safeguarding/education sponsor, source-product/IT owner and privacy/records
reviewer for a synthetic design study. A research/introducer organization and an actual school host
are different roles. The school identifies its existing-vendor gap, one controllable surface and
its existing trained referral path. Participation in a conversation authorizes no data access.

The study begins with invented fixtures and independent review of errors and help-seeking.
Consenting-staff work and any optional limited student exercise need their own protocol and signed
entry gates. Retained cases require a custody decision and implemented rights/holds controls.
No automatic discipline, universal account integration or 24-hour human response service is offered.

Success criteria must cover wrongful restriction, missed concerns, help-seeking completion,
attribution/appeals, participation/trust and total human/infrastructure cost. Owners choose sample
sizes and acceptable error limits before measurement; fewer reports or rewritten messages alone
do not establish benefit. The [charter](../launch/pilot-charter.md) records these obligations and
all pending owners.

Open-source distribution is an intention. No root license has been selected in this snapshot.
Existing TypeScript packages are distributed through a private authenticated registry. A school
needs an agreed distribution/support arrangement; repository visibility alone is not a license,
free service, legal approval or tested self-deployment offer. Pricing and support models remain
hypotheses in the [prospectus](PROSPECTUS.md).

## Page 2 — privacy and operational FAQ

**Which data can it see?** Only an explicitly integrated source supplies submissions. There is no
general access to private LMS messages, personal social accounts or school-wide history. The
initial study uses invented material. Any live source needs source-side authorization and scope.

**Is private student content visible to teachers?** The evaluator is not a teacher case viewer.
Future case access would need tenant/case/purpose authorization, legitimate need and independent
identity disclosure controls. A teacher account alone is insufficient. Source records remain under
the institution's actual record process.

**Does “content-free” mean anonymous?** No. Evaluation/receipt metadata and sparse aggregates can
be linkable. The v2 receiver source includes bounded retention and coarse rollups, but real collection
is disabled and coverage/reporting safeguards are incomplete. No district dashboard is offered.

**Where does submitted content go?** The configured evaluator may call Cloudflare Workers AI Clef.
Provider accounts, direct/Gateway route, logging, exports, caching, agreements and eligible data
classes need actual configuration evidence and human approval. No alternate provider fallback is
introduced. A request flag or synthetic log scan cannot prove provider-wide deletion or ZDR.

**What is retained?** Hall Monitor has bounded keyed idempotency records and content-free receipts.
The original source can retain messages under its own approved schedule. Case records, analytics,
backups and external inference have separate requirements. Approved live retention numbers and
custody remain pending; privacy labels do not erase records or legal/inspection holds.

**Are minors or student images approved?** No new processing is approved by this brief. Grade is
not proof of age or lawful authority. Student image profiles still hold images pending their own
calibration and floor revision. Notices, school agreements and eligibility require responsible owners.

**What if the model, budget or dashboard fails?** Required student moderation retains its fail-closed
behavior. Deterministic support and the source's independently available help route must remain
reachable. The school supplies qualified referral coverage, tested after-hours fallback and real
acknowledgment ownership. Worker availability is not a staffed safeguarding service.

**Can families challenge records?** The source/institution must name inspection, correction, appeal,
multi-student redaction and deletion/hold procedures. A future retained-case service must implement
and test these before enrollment. No live case database or rights workflow is claimed today.

**What can we inspect now?** The [capability matrix](capability-matrix.md), synthetic fixtures,
[custody decision record](../privacy/record-custody-decision.md),
[provider-retention research](../privacy/zero-data-retention.md) and launch checklist. This draft
is not a compliance certification or evidence that a school has agreed to participate.
