# Ranked Michigan pilot prospects and named outreach paths
**Research verified October 28, 2026. This is a prospecting hypothesis, NOT evidence of purchase intent, open RFP, consent or existing customer relationships.**

## Ranking method

Compare organizations on five qualitative factors, *not invented deal-close percentages*: (1) explicit alignment with student wellbeing/relational aggression, (2) public evidence of willingness/mandate to pilot technology, (3) identified appropriate decision-maker, (4) likely practical route to a lawful school-controlled 9–12 communication host, and (5) potential regional distribution. Regulatory/procurement friction and the absence of a verified writable chat integration are disqualifiers until discussed.

**Important:** rank is for **first useful prospect conversation**, not the likelihood that each institution will authorize real student monitoring. A research intermediary may rank #1 but have zero ability to let us ingest student communications from a particular member school.

If none does, move initial pilot to a controlled product such as an EducateOS/PlayTrek discussion module with explicit institutional permission.

## Qualification: six questions on the first call

1. “Which specific *school-managed* collaboration/chat/group activities create the most bullying reports or wrongful moderation?”
2. “How do students submit reports, how many staff-hours review them, and what happens outside school hours?”
3. “Which vendor is already covering Google/Microsoft/Canvas? What is the uncovered behavior or workflow?”
4. “Could we run a controlled, disclosed pilot discussion activity rather than access existing private accounts?”
5. “What approval path covers the superintendent/head, safeguarding team, privacy officer, school board, parents and students?”
6. “What result would make this worth extending—lower false blocks, a legitimate group access restored, easier help-seeking, staff workflow, verified privacy compliance—and who would own the evaluation?”

## What the ranking cannot tell us

- No prior conversation with any institution, no verified procurement RFP and no confirmed contract availability.
- Personal familiarity, alumni networks or local proximity can materially change the order but have not been assumed in the score.
- We do not know a partner's real review coverage, willingness to pay, cyberbullying prevalence, student attitudes toward monitored chat, or own platform API permissions.
- Past public reports/mission statements prove the institution *cares about a topic*, not that it wants a new safety vendor.
- A high ranking should trigger **conversation**, not real student data collection.
