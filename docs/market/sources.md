## Research quality and uncertainty notes

- School enrollment from different school years is a **rough combined ceiling**, not a direct 2026 student census.
- No cited source gives national annual market **revenue** for the proposed category. Our TAM is an **annual seat price sensitivity** and SAM/SOM are unvalidated scenarios, not investment-bank market research.
- Published vendor prices and features change; contracts may be bundled or negotiated. The procurement buyer may be a software platform, not a district.
- Institutional mission statements and contact rosters support outreach relevance, NOT an inference of purchase intent. No acceptance, intro or written pilot consent exists.
- Under-13, FERPA, community trust and open-source licensing are not solved by default “self-hosted” or “ZDR” labels.
- Some upstream research links are PDFs; those have separate pagination. All pricing and numerical use must be reconciled against the displayed table/page before external quoting.
- Review competitive source terms and law again on the actual publication/procurement date; maintain source IDs/retrieval dates in any external investor/board slide.
