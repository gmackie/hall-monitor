# Record custody decision

W1-2026-10-29 · HM-19, with future HM-25/HM-26 and GATE-4 · **Unresolved for live cases**

Original messages, membership authority and authoritative student identity remain with the source
product/institution. Hall Monitor's current evaluator stores content-free receipts and keyed
request digests, not a safeguarding case file. A derived assertion or pseudonym may still be a
protected, reidentifiable student record. The [governance analysis](ferpa-coppa.md) is research,
not a legal determination for a particular institution.

## Decision to obtain before live persistence

| Option | Custodian and implementation boundary | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Source-owned cases | Source/district stores case evidence, rights and identity; Hall Monitor returns transient observations | Reuses a real records system; requires source support for review, correction, audit, holds and access separation |
| Separate district-owned safety service | District designates a custodian; separate restricted service receives narrowly authorized evidence/pointers | More isolation and explicit workflow; requires a new rights/holds/security/operations implementation and qualified staff |
| No retained cases in this study | Existing staffed referral receives concerns under its own policy; demo remains synthetic | Smallest initial scope; does not supply historical cases, investigations or protected case analytics |

Current selection: **no live case persistence is authorized**. These are alternatives for an
accountable district records decision, not three simultaneously implemented stores. The local
synthetic study can proceed. Do not provision a live evidence database based on this document.

## Required responsibility record

For either retained-case option, record a named responsible institution and owner for each row.
All are pending. Access is tenant/case/purpose scoped, with independent disclosure approval.

| Responsibility | Required decision / evidence |
|---|---|
| Record inventory and official owner | Originals, reports, model assertions, counterevidence, corrections, identity mapping and derived copies |
| Lawful authority and purpose | Controller/custodian, school-official/direct-control terms, notices/consents and approved participants |
| Source versus inference | Provenance and uncertainty preserved; an assertion is not a verified fact or disciplinary finding |
| Qualified case review | Trained reviewer, hours, urgent fallback, access audit and purpose limitation |
| Identity resolution | Requester cannot self-approve; minimum necessary scope, expiry, reason and independently audited emergency path |
| Inspection/export | Responsible contact, jurisdictional deadline, multi-student segregation/redaction and delivery verification |
| Correction/appeal/retraction | Authoritative change, downstream invalidation, wrong-attribution repair, review and audit history |
| Retention and preservation | Approved record-specific schedule; deletion authority; inspection/legal holds and release authority |
| Copies and recovery | Indexes, caches, exports, backups, restoration controls, subprocessor duties and verified propagation |
| Withdrawal and incident response | Stop new delivery, revoke access and routes, preserve lawful holds, verify remaining copies and notify under approved procedure |

Do not invent a universal FERPA TTL, infer age from grade, let classroom staff lower privacy
controls, or use classifier confidence as authority to disclose identity. Hold authority and
deletion authority must be reconciled by the records owner before either can run automatically.

## Evidence needed to close the decision

A signed decision identifies host/source, selected option, accountable people, purpose, data
classes, locations/subprocessors, approved durations, rights and urgent response process, technical
control tests, expiry/review date and links to the exact implementation revisions. Privacy,
records, safeguarding and security owners approve their parts. HM-26 remains gated until this
exists; synthetic assertion work can continue without a production case store.
